# AuthGuard-X — 5-Minute SOC Analyst Demo Guide

This reproducible walkthrough demonstrates the complete AuthGuard-X workflow: starting the platform, logging in as a SOC analyst, importing structured security telemetry, running detection correlation, investigating an incident case, and exporting a finalized investigation report.

---

## Prerequisites & Launch

1. Open a terminal in the project directory:
   ```bash
   cd c:\Users\Mannan\OneDrive\Desktop\AuthGuard-X
   ```

2. Start the AuthGuard-X backend:
   ```bash
   python backend/app.py
   ```
   *Expected output:* Server running on `http://127.0.0.1:5000/`.

3. Open your browser and navigate to:
   ```
   http://127.0.0.1:5000/
   ```

---

## Step 1: Authentication & Workspace Overview (Minute 1)

1. On the login screen, click **"Use Demo Credentials"** or enter:
   - **Username:** `admin` (or `analyst_john` / `testadmin`)
   - **Password:** `admin123` (or `AnalystPassword2026!` / `AdminPassword2026!`)
2. Click **Sign In**.
3. Review the **SOC Dashboard Overview**:
   - Inspect the **Health Score** gauge (dynamic KPI reflecting unresolved threat risks).
   - Review the **Severity Breakdown** (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
   - Inspect the **Threat Activity Feed** showing recent identity attacks.

---

## Step 2: Log Ingestion & Telemetry Preprocessing (Minute 2)

1. In the sidebar, click **Security Log Ingestion** (or **Event Explorer**).
2. Click the **"Load Training Dataset"** dropdown and select:
   - **Windows Security Event ID 4625** (Failed Logon Telemetry).
3. Click **Load Sample Telemetry**.
4. Observe the ingestion summary:
   - Total records ingested and normalized to UTC.
   - Batch SHA-256 source hash generated for forensic chain of custody.
5. In the **Normalized Security Events** table, click on any event row to inspect the **Event Detail Modal**:
   - Note the normalized attributes (`Event Time (UTC)`, `Category: Authentication`, `Outcome: Failure`).
   - Observe the **Raw Record SHA-256 Hash** verifying source integrity.

---

## Step 3: Threat Correlation & Alert Triage (Minute 3)

1. Click **Detection Alerts** in the sidebar.
2. Observe the high-fidelity correlated alerts generated by the sliding-window engine (e.g., `RULE-AUTH-001: High-Velocity Credential Stuffing` or `RULE-AUTH-003: Multi-Account Password Spraying`).
3. Click **View Details** on an alert:
   - Inspect the **Detection Narrative** explaining the correlation window and event count.
   - Click the **MITRE ATT&CK Technique Link** (`T1110.001 - Password Spraying`) to review the mapped adversary tactic.
4. Click **"Escalate to Investigation Case"**:
   - The alert automatically provisions a formal case in the SOC queue with linked incident telemetry.

---

## Step 4: Incident Investigation Workspace (Minute 4)

1. Click **Case Management & Investigation** in the sidebar.
2. In your active queue, select the newly escalated case.
3. Advance the **Investigation Lifecycle Stepper**:
   - Move from **New** to **Investigating** (case is claimed and assigned to your account).
4. In the **Case Investigation Workspace**:
   - Add an **Analyst Hypothesis**: `"Observed repeated authentication failures across multiple enterprise identities from single external IP."`
   - Link an **Evidence Pointer**: Add an IOC hash or network reference (e.g., `https://intel.internal/ioc/198.51.100.22`).
5. Progress the case status to **Contained** (e.g., `"Account lockout policy applied and source IP isolated"`).
6. Progress the case status to **Resolved**.

---

## Step 5: Report Generation, Finalization & Export (Minute 5)

1. Click **Case Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"Create Report"** for your resolved case:
   - Title: `"Incident Case #N — Credential Spraying Analysis"`.
3. In the **Report Workspace**, review the auto-populated evidence-grounded facts:
   - **Investigation Timeline**: Chronological fusion of telemetry occurrences, detection trigger times, and analyst containment actions.
   - **Deterministic Quality Indicators (QC)**: Verify that the 6 deterministic indicators (classification, latency, rule coverage, findings, evidence traceability, containment milestone) show `PASS`.
4. Click **Submit for Review**, then click **Approve & Finalize**:
   - A permanent, immutable snapshot is committed to `report_versions`.
5. Export your final documentation:
   - Click **Export HTML** to open the self-contained, printable executive summary with confidentiality markings and `@media print` layout.
   - Click **Export JSON** to download the structured, sanitized JSON representation ready for SIEM/SOAR archival.
